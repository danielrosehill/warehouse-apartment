# Sourcing

The hardest part of the whole system. Not the stacking or the moving — **buying the
gear.** The industrial-storage ecosystem is built for **B2B**: suppliers sell
pallet-quantities to warehouses, not mixed handfuls to households.

## Barriers

- **B2B-only posture** — no consumer webshop, minimums quoted in pallets.
- **Minimum order quantities** — you may have to buy a **half-pallet** of one box
  size to order at all.
- **Near-nonexistent secondhand market** — I couldn't find used Euroboxes. Likely a
  testament to how long they last (buyers keep them).
- **Import route** — Alibaba + **LCL (less-than-container-load) consolidation** works
  for a mixed shipment, but it's a project of its own.

## What worked

Persistence. Politely pushing B2B suppliers until one sold to me — asking for a
half-pallet, being clear on what I wanted, buying in their units rather than mine.

## Sourcing checklist

1. **Search in trade terms** — "Euro container," "KLT box," "stacking container,"
   "picking bin," "Gastronorm" — not "storage box."
2. **Target storage/logistics/catering suppliers**, not consumer retailers.
3. **Buy in their units** — a half-pallet of one size is often the entry price.
4. **Ask directly** — many B2B suppliers will sell to an individual if you ask.
5. **Consider import** (Alibaba + LCL) only for large quantities, with patience.
